HUONG DAN TIKTOK LIVE STUDIO

KHOI DONG
Chay run.bat o thu muc goc. Bridge mac dinh dung http://127.0.0.1:3000.
Neu trinh duyet chua mo, vao http://127.0.0.1:3000/control.html.
Bam Mo game o goc tren ben phai de mo cua so game.
Tab Ket noi LIVE: bat LIVE tren TikTok, nhap username dang phat roi bam Ket noi.
De dung TikFinity du phong, dat LIVE_PROVIDER=tikfinity trong TikTokBridge/.env
va khoi dong lai Bridge.

CAC TAB
- Assets gom cac muc sau:
  - Nhan vat, Canh, Bang hieu: them asset va bam Test de thu trong game.
    Nhan vat ho tro GIF/WebP dong, PNG va JPG.
  - Am nhac: tai MP3/WAV/OGG, chon Phat trong game va chinh am luong.
  - Video man DJ: chon song nhac LED, video MP4 hoac tat man DJ.
  - Phong nen: chon san khau 3D hoac anh nen tai len.
- Cai dat tuong tac: dieu kien tham gia, luat chat/gift va hieu ung.
- Kiem thu / Su kien: demo, gift va hanh dong mau.
- Thong ke phien: xem thong ke va reset.

DU LIEU VA SAO LUU
Du lieu nguoi dung nam trong Data; khong dat nhac/video vao Build.
Sao luu ca thu muc Data khi chuyen may hoac thay ban build.
MediaSources chua GIF va video nguon. UnityProject/Assets/Resources chua asset Unity da dong goi.
Xem docs/PROJECT_STRUCTURE.md de biet duong dan va cach build.
